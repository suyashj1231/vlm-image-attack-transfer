==========================================================================
  TAKEAWAY
==========================================================================
  * CONTROL (exact forced output) is real but white-box: it dies across model
    size, architecture, and simple preprocessing.
  * UltraBreak's transfer is CONTENT: its patterns become text the model READS.
    Reproduced with the authors' own code on Qwen2-VL — 0% control, but the
    target leaks into answers on unseen models.
  * A lenient LLM judge counts that as success; a strict metric does not —
    same images, two metrics: reconciles ICLR'25 'no transfer' with ICLR'26.
